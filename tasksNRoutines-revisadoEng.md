# Brainy tasks and routines — revised proposal

Editorial version for review before updating `brainy-tasks-steps.json` and
`brainy-daily-routines.json`, which remain the product’s sources of truth.

## Editorial guidelines applied

- Natural, neutral English without robotic or overly formal wording.
- One clear action per step.
- The first step can always be done right away, without relying on something
  that should have been prepared the day before.
- Instructions the interface should handle—such as “open Brainy” or “mark the
  habit as complete”—have been removed.
- Steps that involve putting the phone away come last, so users do not lose
  access to the rest of the sequence.
- Larger tasks begin with a low-friction action and end with a clear next step,
  rather than demanding that everything be finished at once.
- Time estimates are guidelines, not deadlines or measures of performance.
- The copy avoids medical claims and absolute promises.

## Revised current tasks

### 🧠 Focus and productivity

#### 📝 Make progress on that report or essay

`escribir_informe` · difficulty: High · metric: `words_written`

1. Open the document and add a working title (2 min).
2. Summarize what you want to communicate in one sentence (3 min).
3. Write down three ideas without worrying about the order (5 min).
4. Put the ideas in the simplest order that makes sense (5 min).
5. Develop only the first idea into a paragraph (15 min).
6. Write one more paragraph, or leave a note showing exactly where to pick up (15 min).
7. Write the introduction once the main body feels clearer (10 min).
8. Do a quick clarity check and save the next action for later (10 min).

#### 📄 Update my résumé and submit an application

`armar_cv_postular` · difficulty: Medium · metric: `applications_sent`

1. Find the most recent version of your résumé (3 min).
2. Make a copy so you can edit it without losing the original (2 min).
3. Update only your most recent job or education entry (10 min).
4. Choose one specific opportunity to apply for (10 min).
5. Tailor the headline and the three most relevant lines to that opportunity (10 min).
6. Write a short message you can reuse (5 min).
7. Attach the files and check your name, email, and links (5 min).
8. Send the application, then decide whether you have the energy for another (5 min).

#### 📚 Start studying for an exam or certification

`estudiar_examen` · difficulty: Medium · metric: `minutes_studied`

1. Open the syllabus and choose just one topic for today (5 min).
2. Turn that topic into a question you want to be able to answer (3 min).
3. Keep only the material you need in view (2 min).
4. Study one section for 20 minutes (20 min).
5. Close the material and write down three ideas you remember (5 min).
6. Try to answer your original question in your own words (5 min).
7. Note what still feels unclear without trying to solve all of it now (3 min).
8. Write down the exact point where you will continue next time (2 min).

### 🏠 Personal organization

#### 👕 Tidy one part of my closet or room

`ordenar_closet` · difficulty: Medium

1. Choose a small area: one drawer, one shelf, or one surface (2 min).
2. Set up three spots: keep, remove, and decide later (3 min).
3. Take out only the items from that area (5 min).
4. Sort each item without reorganizing the rest of the room (10 min).
5. Put back what you want to keep (10 min).
6. Place anything leaving your home in a bag (5 min).
7. Put uncertain items in a box and add a date to revisit them (3 min).
8. Stop here and choose another area for a different day (2 min).

#### 💸 Check my finances without getting overwhelmed

`poner_al_dia_finanzas` · difficulty: Medium

1. Open your main account and check the available balance (3 min).
2. Write down any outstanding payments you remember (5 min).
3. Add the due date and amount for the most urgent ones (10 min).
4. Choose one based on urgency, due date, or late fees (3 min).
5. Pay it or schedule the payment (10 min).
6. Save the receipt somewhere easy to find (2 min).
7. Move on to another only if you still have the energy (10 min).
8. Schedule a short check-in for anything still outstanding (2 min).

#### 📑 Make progress on that pending form or errand

`resolver_tramite` · difficulty: Medium

1. Write the outcome you need in one sentence (2 min).
2. Find the information on the official website or source (10 min).
3. Copy the requirements into a short checklist (5 min).
4. Mark what you already have and what is still missing (5 min).
5. Get only the first missing document (15 min).
6. Book the appointment or complete the first part of the form (15 min).
7. Save the documents and confirmations in one folder (5 min).
8. Note the next step, including the date, place, and time if needed (3 min).

### 💪 Health and energy

#### 💪 Start moving without pushing myself too hard

`empezar_rutina_ejercicio` · difficulty: Medium · metric: `workouts_completed`

1. Choose one simple type of movement that feels right for you (3 min).
2. Decide where you will do it today (1 min).
3. Put on comfortable clothes and shoes (5 min).
4. Start with two minutes of gentle movement (2 min).
5. Continue for five more minutes (5 min).
6. Decide whether to keep going or stop for today; both choices count (1 min).
7. Note how you felt and when you might like to do it again (2 min).

#### 🍱 Get a few meals ready for the week

`meal_prep` · difficulty: Medium

1. Choose two simple meals you would actually want to eat (10 min).
2. Check which ingredients you already have (5 min).
3. Write down only what is missing (5 min).
4. Buy or order those ingredients (20 min).
5. Clear one part of the kitchen to work in (5 min).
6. Wash and prep the ingredients both meals have in common (15 min).
7. Cook the easier option first (30 min).
8. Portion and store it; leave the second recipe for another time if you need to (10 min).

#### 🌅 Set up a morning with fewer decisions

`rutina_matutina` · difficulty: Medium

1. Choose three actions you want to repeat after waking up (5 min).
2. Put them in the easiest order to remember (3 min).
3. Leave water and one essential item somewhere visible (3 min).
4. Choose what you will wear the next morning (5 min).
5. Set an alarm that makes you sit up or get out of bed to turn it off (3 min).
6. Try the sequence for one morning without expecting it to be perfect (10 min).
7. Remove or simplify the step that created the most friction (5 min).

### 😌 Calm and emotional regulation

#### 🧠 Get everything that is pending out of my head

`vaciar_cabeza` · difficulty: Low

1. Choose paper or a digital note—whichever is closest (1 min).
2. Add the title “What’s taking up space in my mind” (1 min).
3. Write everything down for five minutes without organizing or editing (5 min).
4. Stop when the time is up, even if there is more to add (1 min).
5. Circle the three items that need the most attention (3 min).
6. Choose one and turn it into a small, concrete action (5 min).
7. Save the rest for later without trying to solve it now (1 min).

#### 🌙 Set myself up for a calmer evening

`rutina_noche` · difficulty: Low

1. Choose an approximate time to start winding down (3 min).
2. Pick three simple actions to close out the day (5 min).
3. Dim the lights when that time arrives (2 min).
4. Prepare one thing that will make the next morning easier (5 min).
5. Choose a short, calming activity to end the day (5 min).
6. Turn on Do Not Disturb and leave your phone out of reach as the final step (2 min).

#### ⚖️ Make a decision I have been putting off

`tomar_decision` · difficulty: Medium

1. Write the decision as one clear sentence (3 min).
2. Decide when you need to have made it (2 min).
3. Narrow the options down to no more than three (5 min).
4. Choose the criterion that matters most right now (3 min).
5. Remove any option that does not meet that criterion (5 min).
6. Choose an option that is good enough, not perfect (3 min).
7. Take the first reversible step toward that decision (5 min).

## Revised current routines

The steps describe the daily action. The app handles completion tracking, so
“mark as complete” is not included as a step in any routine.

### 🏠 Personal organization

#### 💧 Start the day with water and light

`hidratacion_anclaje` · every day

**Why:** Repeating a short sequence after waking reduces the number of decisions
you need to make and gives the day a familiar starting point.

1. Pour or pick up a glass of water.
2. Drink it before moving on to another activity.
3. If you have medication prescribed for this time, take it as directed by your healthcare professional.
4. Open the curtains or spend a few minutes near natural light.
5. Choose one gentle action to begin your day.

#### 🧹 Tidy one area for five minutes

`orden_5_min` · every day

**Why:** Focusing on one specific area for a short amount of time makes tidying
feel finishable instead of like an endless project.

1. Choose one surface or small area.
2. Set aside five minutes for that area.
3. Put away anything that clearly belongs somewhere else.
4. Remove any visible trash, cups, or dishes.
5. Stop when the time is up, even if the area is not perfect.

### 💪 Health and energy

#### ☀️ Get a few minutes of natural light

`luz_solar` · every day

**Why:** Adding natural light near the beginning of the day can create a clear
cue that it is time to shift into your daily activities.

1. Open the curtains or find the brightest available spot.
2. Stand by a window or go outside if you can.
3. Stay there for about five minutes.
4. Breathe slowly or gently move your body while you wait.
5. When you are done, choose the next simple action in your morning.

#### 🥚 Make a breakfast that keeps me going

`desayuno_energia` · every day

**Why:** Having a simple, repeatable option reduces decisions and keeps making
breakfast from becoming another overwhelming task.

1. See which simple option is available today.
2. Add a source of protein if that is part of the way you normally eat.
3. Make the simplest version of that breakfast.
4. Sit down and spend the first few minutes eating without doing another task.

#### 🏃 Move for a few minutes

`movimiento_corto` · every day

**Why:** Starting with just a few minutes lowers the barrier to getting started
and lets movement count even on low-energy days.

1. Choose the easiest kind of movement for today.
2. Set aside five to ten minutes.
3. Start with one gentle minute without aiming for intensity.
4. Continue until you reach the amount of time you chose.
5. Stop or keep going a little longer based on how you feel.

#### 🧴 Do my basic personal care

`cuidado_personal` · every day

**Why:** A small, consistent sequence reduces how many decisions it takes to
start caring for yourself.

1. Go to the bathroom and begin with whichever action feels easiest.
2. Brush your teeth.
3. Wash your face or take a shower, depending on what you need today.
4. Do your chosen care step: moisturizer, sunscreen, serum, shaving, or beard care.
5. Keep the products together so they are easy to find next time.


### 🧠 Focus and productivity

#### 🎯 Choose three realistic priorities

`top_3_dia` · every day

**Why:** Limiting your attention to three priorities reduces competition between
unfinished tasks and makes it clearer what needs to happen first.

1. Look at what you have pending today.
2. Choose one necessary task, one important task, and one optional task.
3. Put them in the order that makes the most sense to start.
4. Write the first physical, concrete action for your main priority.
5. Spend two minutes starting that action.

#### 🧘 Take a short screen break

`pausa_pantallas` · every day

**Why:** A short pause can reduce overload and make it easier to return with a
clearer intention.

1. Finish the sentence or action you are doing now.
2. Move the screen out of your line of sight.
3. Look into the distance, stretch, or get some water.
4. Keep the break between five and ten minutes.
5. Before returning, choose the one action you will do next.

### 😌 Calm and emotional regulation

#### 📝 Clear my head before bed

`brain_dump_nocturno` · every day

**Why:** Moving unfinished thoughts out of your head helps you close the day
without trying to solve everything before sleep.

1. Choose paper or a digital note.
2. Spend five minutes writing down anything still on your mind.
3. Mark only what needs attention tomorrow.
4. Turn one of those items into a small action.
5. Close the note and leave the rest for another time.

#### 🌙 End the day with less stimulation

`rutina_cierre_sueno` · every day

**Why:** Repeating a calming sequence can make it easier to shift from the day’s
activity into a time for rest, with fewer decisions to make.

1. Dim the main lights in the room.
2. Prepare one thing you will need tomorrow.
3. Do your basic nighttime hygiene.
4. Choose a calming activity: reading, breathing, or stretching.
5. Turn on Do Not Disturb.
6. Leave your phone out of reach as the final step.

## Proposed new tasks

These tasks require new IDs and must first be added to
`brainy-tasks-steps.json`. They are not part of the current contract until they
have been approved and integrated.

### 🧠 Focus and productivity

#### ✉️ Reply to that message or email I have been avoiding

Proposed ID: `responder_mensaje_pendiente` · difficulty: Low · proposed metric: `messages_sent`

1. Open only the conversation you have been avoiding (2 min).
2. Read the latest message once (3 min).
3. Write one sentence describing what the other person needs from you (2 min).
4. Draft a short reply with a greeting, your answer, and the next step (5 min).
5. Remove any explanation that is not needed (2 min).
6. Check the recipient and any attachments (2 min).
7. Send the message before editing it again (1 min).

### 🏠 Personal organization

#### 🗓️ Plan my week without filling every moment

Proposed ID: `organizar_semana_realista` · difficulty: Medium

1. Look at the commitments that already have a date (5 min).
2. Put everything else in a temporary list (5 min).
3. Choose three important outcomes for the week (5 min).
4. Give each one a likely day without filling every open space (5 min).
5. Break the first one into an action that takes less than 15 minutes (5 min).
6. Leave at least one open block for the unexpected (2 min).
7. Decide which pending task you will not do this week (3 min).

### 💪 Health and energy

#### 🩺 Book or prepare for a health appointment

Proposed ID: `preparar_cita_salud` · difficulty: Medium

1. Write down the type of care you need (2 min).
2. Look up one available clinic or healthcare professional (10 min).
3. Choose a date you can realistically make (5 min).
4. Book the appointment or prepare the request (10 min).
5. Save the confirmation and address (3 min).
6. Write down up to three things you want to discuss (5 min).
7. Set a reminder for the day before (2 min).

### 😌 Calm and emotional regulation

#### 💬 Prepare for a conversation I have been avoiding

Proposed ID: `preparar_conversacion` · difficulty: Medium

1. Write what you need to communicate in one sentence (3 min).
2. Separate what you observed from what you are assuming (5 min).
3. Note how it made you feel and what you need now (5 min).
4. Define one specific, reasonable request (5 min).
5. Choose a sensible time to talk (3 min).
6. Practice a short, respectful opening (5 min).
7. Send a message to arrange it or begin the conversation (3 min).

## Proposed new routines

These routines require new IDs and must first be added to
`brainy-daily-routines.json`.

### 🧠 Focus and productivity

#### 🚀 Start with two minutes

Proposed ID: `arranque_dos_minutos` · every day

**Why:** Reducing a task to one tiny first action means getting started requires
fewer decisions and less preparation.

1. Choose a task you have been avoiding.
2. Define one action you can do in under two minutes.
3. Do only that action.
4. Decide whether to continue or stop; getting started already counts.

### 🏠 Personal organization

#### 🌤️ Set up tomorrow’s first step

Proposed ID: `primer_paso_manana` · every day

**Why:** Deciding on the first step ahead of time reduces the friction of
getting started the next day.

1. Choose one priority for tomorrow.
2. Write it as a concrete action, not a complete project.
3. Leave the first item or file you will need somewhere visible.
4. Remove one obvious distraction from that space.

### 💪 Health and energy

#### 💧 Take a water and movement break

Proposed ID: `pausa_agua_movimiento` · every day

**Why:** A short break combines two easy actions and creates a reset point in
the middle of the day.

1. Stand up from where you are.
2. Drink a glass of water.
3. Move your shoulders, neck, or legs for one minute.
4. Choose the concrete action you will return to.

### 😌 Calm and emotional regulation

#### 🧩 Reset when I feel stuck

Proposed ID: `reinicio_bloqueo` · every day

**Why:** Naming what is blocking you and shrinking the next step keeps “solve
everything at once” from feeling like the only option.

1. Pause and place both feet on the floor.
2. Name what is holding you back: uncertainty, fatigue, fear, or too many options.
3. Reduce what is pending to the smallest action you can see.
4. Do that action for two minutes.
5. Choose the next step only after those two minutes are over.
